## 🚀 Game Overview & Lore

In the deep orbit above Earth's energy grid, alien drone fleets and radioactive warhead asteroids are launching a coordinated orbital assault. 

Taking command of the **Kiraht-Vanguard Interceptor**, you must hold the perimeter, vaporize enemy waves, harvest radioactive **Uranium Cores**, and charge your **Atomic Reactor Core** to 100%. When primed, detonate the **Atomic Boom** — a massive shockwave that disintegrates every threat on screen in a blinding nuclear flash!

## 🛡️ Key Features

### 1. ☢️ The "Atomic Boom" Superweapon
- **Energy Accumulation**: Destroy enemies and collect green ☢️ **Uranium Fuel** drops to charge the bottom reactor gauge from `0%` to `100%`.
- **Detonation Sequence**: Press `SPACE` or `B` when charged to trigger:
  - Screen-wide blinding nuclear flash
  - Camera shake physics
  - Expanding multi-layered neon shockwave rings
  - Complete vaporization of all non-boss enemies on screen with high score multipliers!

### 2. 👾 Dynamic Enemy Waves
- **Scout Interceptors**: Nimble drones that descend in fast zigzag trajectories.
- **Radioactive Asteroids**: Heavy tumbling rocks that break apart into smaller fragments.
- **Heavy Dreadnoughts**: Armored warships firing targeted red laser projectiles.
- **Titan Boss (Waves 5, 10, ...)**: Colossal command dreadnought featuring multi-stage health bars, heavy plating, and bullet-hell projectile barrages.

### 3. ⚡ Power-Ups & Collectibles
- ☢️ **Uranium Core**: Instantly charges the Atomic Reactor by `+25%`.
- ⚡ **Tri-Shot Spread Cannon**: Upgrades your weapons to a 3-way plasma barrage for 8 seconds.
- 🛡️ **Shield Bubble**: Restores 40% energy to your energy deflector shield.
- ❤️ **Hull Nanobots**: Repairs damaged ship hull integrity.

### 4. 🔊 Procedural Web Audio Engine
- Built **100% with the browser's native Web Audio API**.
- Zero external `.mp3` or `.wav` dependencies — every laser sweep, explosion crunch, shield hum, and sub-bass atomic boom is mathematically synthesized on the fly.
- Includes a one-click audio mute/unmute toggle.

### 5. 🏆 Glassmorphism HUD & High Score Persistence
- Futuristic dark synthwave aesthetic with glowing neon cyan, radioactive green, and deep space obsidian.
- Record high score is saved locally via `localStorage` so your arcade legacy remains intact between sessions.
